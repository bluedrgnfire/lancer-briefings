Good morning Ladies and Gentlemen - it is a tremendous honor to join you today, coming all the way from CENTCOM for the Union Fleet to personally congradulate these 482 Mechanized Infintry Pilots as they graduate from the Union Naval Academy. Over the last 18 months of Navy Training these individuals have received the best training that Union has to offer, immersed in the physical and mental training that it takes to be a mech pilot, that it takes to be a Lancer. They have proven themselves to be disiplined, physically, mentally and emotionally fit and ready to integrate into the protective fighting force of Union.
Before I get too far ahead of myself, I am the Commander of Union Fleet Forces Command, responsible for leading, generating, and employing hundreds of ships, mechanized infantry, and aircraft with the most important resource that brings all of the metal to life – its Pilots and Crew. 
To the Recruits, take a breath – you did it! You are ready to join this elite team. You are the real one percent, the one percent of Union who choose to serve, choose to wear the cloth of our nation. You made a decision to join the profession of arms and become part of something bigger than yourselves… the galaxy’s greatest Navy. And you chose to serve during a time when it is most needed – a critical time in our history – perhaps Union’s most consequential decade since SECCOM. In a universe where the Navy is fighting to attract our country’s best and brightest, as I look out into the crowd – I have renewed faith that we are succeeding – succeeding by attracting the amazing talent that stands before me – ready to fight and win with dedication, courage, and honor. 
As the galaxy's greatest Navy and most responsive Service within the Joint Force, we have the daunting responsibility to remain Union’s away team and mobile fighting force, on watch around the galaxy 24-hours a day, 365 days a year. As you reflect on your time here from ‘P Days’ (processing days) to Battlestations to this culminating event, celebrate that you will no longer be a Recruit – you have earned the title of a Union Mechanized Pilot. You should be extremely proud. I know I am.
To the families… you played an integral role in your Pilot’s development. You shaped your Pilot into someone who wanted to stand up, volunteer and serve our nation. I feel very strongly about the importance of family within the fabric of our Navy community – and today, I am reinvigorated to say with conviction that it is an indisputable fact that family readiness directly contributes to operational readiness – that a stronger family means a stronger Fleet. I know you are proud of their willingness to serve, but I also understand your concern for their well-being as a Pilot in these dynamic times. I’m a Navy parent too, I get it. I have one son-in-law who serves on a Navy Frigate, who is in the Casscadian Range right now, and a Marine Maintenance officer who serves as well, and is another son-in-law. In response to this concern, I ask you this… who better to stand up against our adversaries than our very best? Who better to defend our way of life than our own? Our sons and daughters. Trust me, they’re ready! You raised these amazing, talented young men and women with a strong foundation of Honor, Courage, and Commitment – the Core Values upon which our strength is based and victory is achieved. Because of the strong base you provided, I can think of no better group of patriots to carry on such a proud legacy. Your support, love and guidance cannot end today. While your Sailor is tough, as they have proven time and time again during these last 18 months, they will continue to need you during their journey. They will soon transfer to a ship, a post, or an aircraft squadron where they must hit the ground running with confidence, motivated to learn their job and overcome any obstacle. Our Navy and our Nation demand their very best.
Because their journey will be challenging, I know you will continue to support these young men and women as they start the next chapter of their carrer. Stay in touch, stay connected, demand the most from our Navy to ensure their quality of service is and remains world class. We are honored that these Pilots chose a life of service – we are honored they chose the Navy.
Let me say that we support you, we thank you for your service, and we wish you the very best as you begin this fantastic journey.
